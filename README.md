# Hi, I'm Ngozi 👋

I'm an AI researcher working on natural language processing for African languages.

My work focuses on making large language models work well, and fairly, for low-resource and African languages: how we evaluate them, where they fail, and how to align them with the cultures they are meant to serve. I care about building things that are useful and sharing what I learn along the way.

I also write essays on AI safety, language, and identity on my blog, Perspectives.

I'm a Mastercard Foundation Scholar at AIMS Cameroon.

### What I work on
- LLM evaluation for African languages, and why current benchmarks fall short
- Cultural alignment of language models
- Safety and alignment in low-resource settings
- Data science for social good

### Writing
On [Perspectives](https://ngozi-uzor.github.io/perspectives/index.html) I write about AI safety and governance from the margins, language and code-switching, and African identity. A few pieces:
- Who Does AI Safety Leave Out?
- Benchmarks Lie
- Red Teaming from the Margins
- Code-Switching as a Superpower
